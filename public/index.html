About me.

Hi, I’m Chen-Chieh (Jacky) Liao · 廖振傑, an Assistant Professor at the Institute of Science Tokyo. I am also a Visiting Scholar at Adelaide University and a Guest Researcher at the University of Tokyo.

My research connects human–computer interaction, machine learning, and 3D vision. I build AI and XR systems that make movement understandable and controllable, helping people acquire skills and create expressive motion. My work spans sports and piano training, 3D body and hand pose estimation, multimodal sensing, and interactions with virtual characters and animals.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Institute of Science Tokyo in 2025, advised by Prof. Hideki Koike, and my B.E. and M.S. in Computer Science from Tokyo Institute of Technology in 2020 and 2022.

Previously, I worked as a Researcher at Science Tokyo, an Associate Researcher at Cygames, a Visiting Researcher at Keio University, an AI Researcher at NAVER Cloud, and an Algorithm Engineer at ACES.
